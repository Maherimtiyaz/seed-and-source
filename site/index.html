Every company you admire started as two things.

A seed: an idea someone couldn't stop thinking about, a founder willing to look foolish, a first check, a first customer. Small, buried and easy to miss. By the time a startup is in the headlines, the seed is long out of sight.

And source: the code, the tools and the decisions it grows from. More of it is public than ever, in open repos, changelogs and pricing pages that anyone can read if they know where to dig.

Most startup writing covers one half. Funding news without the code. Engineering posts without the business. Founder myths without the numbers.

Seed & Source digs up both. Each week we take one company, from YC launches to AI startups to the open-source projects quietly becoming businesses, and trace it from the first idea down to the stack. Everything we claim links to where we found it.
