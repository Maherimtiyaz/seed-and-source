Day 0

I · The seed

It starts where nobody is looking.

A founder gets annoyed by a problem that won't go away. They sketch an answer, pitch it, and usually get told no. Nothing about it is visible yet. No logo, no users, no headline.

By the time you read about a company, this part has been buried for years. We go back and dig it up: who was annoyed, by what, and the first version they built.

In every issue The Story where the idea came from

Month 2

II · The roots

Plants grow roots before anyone sees a leaf.

Startups do too. Before the first customer, there is a stack: a database someone picked, an open-source library that saved six months, an engineering decision that will matter for years.

More of this is public than ever, in open repos, changelogs and engineering blogs. We read them and explain the one decision that holds the whole thing up, in plain words.

In every issue Under the Hood the stack and the decision behind it

Month 6

III · The sprout

Then, one morning, something breaks the surface.

The first customers. A launch that finally lands. Sometimes it's a new feature; surprisingly often it's a new sentence that explains the product better than the old one did.

That turning point is the moment founders remember, and the one that's easiest to learn from. We find it, and we show you the proof.

In every issue The Story the moment it clicked

Year 1

IV · The stem

A plant needs something to carry water up.

A company needs something to carry money in. Pricing, the first sales, funding rounds, how big the team got and when. This is the part founders talk about least and readers want most.

We only use numbers the company or reputable press reported. When a figure is an outside estimate, we say so.

In every issue The Business Side how it makes money

Year 5

V · The bloom

The part everyone sees.

The billion-dollar round, the launch on every timeline, the company your friends suddenly use. It looks overnight. It never is.

Every issue ends with what's flowering right now: the AI startups that had a big week, and the launches, rounds and open-source releases worth a look.

In every issue AI Radar + Quick Hits what's blooming this week
